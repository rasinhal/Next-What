# Next-What
Camera-based motion games for kids and adults: your body is the controller, on phone, tablet, PC or TV.

## Games

All games live in one web app in [bubble-pop/](bubble-pop/). Open https://rasinhal.github.io/Next-What/ for the game menu.

- Runner: a three-lane run. Step left or right to change lanes, jump over hurdles, crouch under bars, grab coins.
- Cricket: you bat. Swing your hands like a bat as the ball arrives; faster swings score fours and sixes. 12 balls, 3 wickets.
- Table Tennis: your hand is the paddle. Play the computer, or a friend on the other half of the screen.
- Bubble Pop: pop floating bubbles with your hands.

Every game has three age levels (3 to 6, 7 to 12, 13+) and 1 or 2 players. With 2 players each person stands on their own half of the screen.
