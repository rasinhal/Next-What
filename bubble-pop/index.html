Motion Games

Your body is the controller. Prop up the phone, tablet or laptop, step back, and play.

🏃 Runner Dodge, jump and duck down the track 🏏 Cricket Swing your hands like a bat and hit sixes 🏓 Table Tennis Your hand is the paddle 🍉 Fruit Slice Swipe fast to slice flying fruit 🧤 Goalkeeper Stop the shots with your hands 🔨 Mole Smash Bop the moles before they hide 🫧 Bubble Pop Pop bubbles with your hands

1 or 2 players in every game. Video stays on this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
